# auto-96cb5031
## Diagnosis
`_clampEnvMs('RECODE_BUFFER_MS', ...)` previously let the operator-controlled env var drive the recode buffer. With `RECODE_BUFFER_MS=900000` (15 min) in both `.env` files and `CUA_TOTAL_TIMEOUT_MS=4500000` (75 min), the pre-recode guard threshold was `75 − 15 = 60 min`. Tasks that legitimately spent 61–64 min in CUA rounds were killed at the pre-recode check on `cua-verify.cjs:768` with `"CUA total time limit exceeded (64min > 60min pre-recode guard)"`, wasting all accumulated CUA progress and burning a retry. The fix raises the threshold to 63 min by hardcoding the buffer at 12 min and ignoring the env var.

## Root Cause
`engine/stages/cua-verify.cjs:220` — `RECODE_BUFFER_MS` was formerly resolved through `_clampEnvMs('RECODE_BUFFER_MS', 15*60*1000, ...)`, making 15 min the effective default/env value (`.env RECODE_BUFFER_MS=900000`). Both pre-recode guard sites (line 326 and line 768) evaluated `elapsed > MAX_CUA_TOTAL_MS - RECODE_BUFFER_MS = 3 600 000 ms = 60 min`, triggering on any task whose last completed CUA round finished past the 60-min mark.
