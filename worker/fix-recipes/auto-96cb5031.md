# auto-96cb5031

## Diagnosis
`RECODE_BUFFER_MS=900000` (15 min) in `.env` / `worker/.env` was being consumed by the pipeline process, making the pre-recode guard fire at `MAX_CUA_TOTAL_MS − RECODE_BUFFER_MS = 75min − 15min = 60min`. Any CUA run that crossed the 60-minute mark was thrown as a hard failure even though 15 minutes of recode headroom remained—more than enough for a full recode + rebuild cycle (~8–10 min observed). The fix hardcodes `RECODE_BUFFER_MS = 12 * 60 * 1000` (12 min) directly in the module, raising the guard to 63 min, and suppresses env-var override with a startup warning. The `.env` and `worker/.env` files still carry the now-ignored but stale `RECODE_BUFFER_MS=720000` entry that must be removed to eliminate the misleading startup warning.

## Root Cause
`engine/stages/cua-verify.cjs:220` — `RECODE_BUFFER_MS` was previously sourced from `process.env.RECODE_BUFFER_MS` (900,000 ms = 15 min); with `MAX_CUA_TOTAL_MS = 75 min` the guard threshold was `60 min`, prematurely killing runs that finished a CUA round at ~63–64 min.
